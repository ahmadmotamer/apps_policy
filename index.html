Privacy Policy

Sonbola · Effective October 15, 2026

This policy explains how the following apps by Sonbola (“we”, “us”) handle information:

AppPackage / bundle IDWhat it does
MakePDFcom.sonbola.makepdfTurns photos into PDF documents
Loan Calculatorcom.sonbola.loancalculatorCalculates loan and mortgage payments
Hydrunocom.sonbola.hydrunoTracks how much water you drink and reminds you to drink
Scanoraqcom.sonbola.scanoraqScans and creates QR codes and barcodes
In short
  • The apps have no accounts and no servers of ours. What you create in them (PDFs, drink logs, loan figures, scan history) stays on your device.
  • The apps show ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device and advertising data to show and measure ads, with your consent where the law requires it.
  • We use Google Firebase to understand how the apps are used and to fix crashes.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information that stays on your device

Each app stores what you create in it only on your device, in the app’s private storage. We never receive it.

  • MakePDF: the photos you choose or take for a document, and the PDF files you create.
  • Loan Calculator: the loan amounts, rates and terms you enter.
  • Hydruno: your drink log, daily goal, unit and reminder settings.
  • Scanoraq: your history of scanned and created codes.

Deleting an item in the app, clearing the app’s data, or uninstalling the app removes this information permanently. When you choose to share a file or text, it goes only to the app or person you pick.

2. Device permissions

  • Camera (MakePDF, Scanoraq): used only while you take a photo or scan a code. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded by us.
  • Photos (MakePDF, Scanoraq): the apps use the system photo picker, so they can see only the photos you select.
  • Notifications (Hydruno): used only to show the drink reminders you turn on. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

You can turn any permission off in your device settings at any time.

3. Advertising

The apps show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect from your device:

  • your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, when permitted),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language,
  • how you interact with ads.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a US state with applicable privacy laws, the apps ask for your choices through Google’s consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choices at any time with Settings → Privacy options in the app.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to learn which features are used and to fix problems. They may collect an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, general usage events (for example, “PDF created” or “drink added”) and crash details. Events can include simple details such as a document’s page count or a drink’s size, but never the content you create: your documents and photos, loan figures, or the text of codes you scan or create. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Purchases

If an app offers an optional purchase, such as removing ads, payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was made.

6. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Google AdMob and Google Firebase) for the purposes described, or when required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s own retention policies. Because we do not run servers that hold your data, we have no copy to delete; if you have a request about data processed by our service providers, contact us and we will help, or contact Google directly.

8. Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Most of these you can exercise directly in the apps and your device settings as described above. For anything else, contact us.

10. Security

Your content is kept in each app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. Our service providers protect data in transit using encryption. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date at the top.

12. Contact
